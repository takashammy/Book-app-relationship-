# Soft Uncanny × Luminary framework

**Locked mix:** Surrealism × Pointillism (Soft Uncanny).  
**Locked chassis:** Luminary’s editorial UI.  
**Locked rule:** type, grain, and structure only — **no pictures**. No cups, doors, keys, moons, keyholes, feathers, or any other illustration.

Luminary’s formatting: 4-column poster grid (bleed · vertical spine · content · bleed), rotated English spines beside upright Chinese, volume index (`01`), uppercase tracked badges (`DAILY READ | RELATIONSHIPS`), duration/theme spines, numbered modules, bilingual essay, footer switcher. Paper and Swiss charcoal are replaced by stipple grain in dusk rose / pollen / grain-blue / mist.

## What is locked (Luminary)

- Inset 1px frame + outer registration bleed.
- Left ~58px spine: vertical-rl English, upright Chinese.
- Row 1: volume numeral + `DAILY READ |` category, split by a hairline.
- Row 2–3: `DURATION` spine, large serif title, `THEME` spine, pull-quote, thesis.
- Modules numbered `01` `02` `03` (and `04` for practice).
- Technical labels in uppercase grotesque, wide tracking.
- Editorial prose in a readable serif; Chinese in Song/Ming at the same rank.
- Footer: Read · Library · Calendar · You, plus a next-volume line on the reader.

## What is Soft Uncanny (now type-only)

- Field is pointillist grain (mist, grain-blue, dusk rose, pollen) — not newsprint `#FAF6EE`.
- Ink is warm stone `#3A3532`, not poster charcoal.
- Atmosphere comes from the stipple, not from objects.
- No hearts, no dating chrome, no Blinkist finish-rings, no clay plates, no illustrations.

## The five screens

**1. Reader.** Volume `01`, title *The Room We Keep* / 我们留下的房间. Duration and theme spines. Quote + thesis. Empty grain in the lower field.

**2. Chapter summary.** Same frame. `02 CHAPTER SUMMARY`. Three numbered principles (Stay, Repair, Silence) with a one-line plain-language gloss each.

**3. Library.** Same frame. Category chips as uppercase badges. A short list of volumes with index numbers and `LOGGED` marks. Status is type only.

**4. Calendar.** Same frame. Month grid, today as a pollen typographic mark or dot — not an object. Day inspector under the grid (volume logged + a one-line note).

**5. Resonance + practice.** Same frame. Classical Chinese quote with a modern gloss, then `04` a single daily experiment and an empty checkbox.

These are stills, not a build. Next useful step is one high-fidelity reader with real type and real grid.
