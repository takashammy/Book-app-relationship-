## Category 01: ATTACHMENT & BONDING SCIENCE (`依附理論與親密連結 /`)

1. **001. Attached** — Amir Levine & Rachel Heller (2010) | *TC:* 依附 | *SC:* 依恋
   *Core Premise:* Translates adult attachment science (Secure, Anxious, Avoidant) into practical tools for identifying protest behaviors and building a secure base.
2. **002. Hold Me Tight** — Sue Johnson (2008) | *TC:* 抱緊我 | *SC:* 抱紧我
   *Core Premise:* Introduces Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), showing how adult fights are disguised cries for attachment safety ("Are you there for me?").
3. **003. A Secure Base** — John Bowlby (1988) | *TC:* 安全基地 | *SC:* 安全基地
   *Core Premise:* Foundational clinical lectures by the father of attachment theory explaining how early caregiving templates shape lifelong emotional security.
4. **004. Wired for Love** — Stan Tatkin (2011) | *TC:* 為愛連線 | *SC:* 为爱连线
   *Core Premise:* Combines interpersonal neurobiology and attachment theory to help partners act as mutual nervous-system regulators ("couple bubble").
5. **005. Polysecure** — Jessica Fern (2020) | *TC:* 多元安全依附 | *SC:* 多元安全依附
   *Core Premise:* Extends attachment theory beyond traditional monogamy, introducing the "HEARTS" framework for cultivating earned security in any relationship structure.
6. **006. The Power of Attachment** — Diane Poole Heller (2019) | *TC:* 依附的力量 | *SC:* 依附的力量
   *Core Premise:* Provides somatic and mindfulness exercises to heal disorganized and insecure attachment wounds into "Earned Secure Attachment."
7. **007. Anxiously Attached** — Jessica Baum (2022) | *TC:* 焦慮依附療癒 | *SC:* 焦虑依恋疗愈
   *Core Premise:* Guides anxious attachers through self-soothing, reparenting, and setting clear emotional standards instead of chasing unavailable partners.
8. **008. Insecure in Love** — Leslie Becker-Phelps (2014) | *TC:* 愛情裡的不安全感 | *SC:* 爱情里的不安全感
   *Core Premise:* Uses Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) and compassionate self-awareness to quiet anxious attachment rumination.
9. **009. Avoidant: How to Love (or Leave) a Dismissive Partner** — Jeb Kinnison (2014) | *TC:* 逃避型戀人 | *SC:* 回避型恋人
   *Core Premise:* Examines the psychological roots of dismissive-avoidant distancing and outlines realistic boundaries for partners caught in push-pull dynamics.
10. **010. Attachment in Psychotherapy** — David J. Wallin (2007) | *TC:* 心理治療中的依附 | *SC:* 心理治疗中的依恋
    *Core Premise:* Explores how unspoken bodily and nonverbal attachment patterns replay in adult intimacy and how reflective functioning heals them.
11. **011. Love Sense** — Sue Johnson (2013) | *TC:* 愛的科學感知 | *SC:* 爱的科学感知
    *Core Premise:* Presents neuroimaging and clinical evidence proving romantic love is an evolutionary survival bond rather than an irrational chemical accident.
12. **012. Secure Love** — Julie Menanno (2024) | *TC:* 安全之愛 | *SC:* 安全之爱
    *Core Premise:* Maps out step-by-step dialogue scripts to interrupt negative attachment cycles between anxious pursuers and avoidant withdrawers.
13. **013. Attachment Theory in Practice** — Sue Johnson (2019) | *TC:* 依附理論實務 | *SC:* 依恋理论实务
    *Core Premise:* Breaks down the core emotional transformations required to turn rigid defensive armor into vulnerable relational bonding.
14. **014. Becoming Attached** — Robert Karen (1994) | *TC:* 依附的形成 | *SC:* 依恋的形成
    *Core Premise:* Narrative history of John Bowlby and Mary Ainsworth's landmark research into how human infants and adults form enduring emotional bonds.
15. **015. Wired for Dating** — Stan Tatkin (2016) | *TC:* 為約會連線 | *SC:* 为约会连线
    *Core Premise:* Applies neuroscience and attachment principles to early courtship, helping daters spot red flags and build mutual trust from day one.
16. **016. The Attachment Theory Workbook** — Annie Chen (2019) | *TC:* 依附理論練習手冊 | *SC:* 依恋理论练习手册
    *Core Premise:* Practical diagnostic exercises for identifying triggers, core relational fears, and concrete communication habits for each attachment style.
17. **017. Healing Your Attachment Wounds** — Diane Poole Heller (2012) | *TC:* 療癒你的依附創傷 | *SC:* 疗愈你的依附创伤
    *Core Premise:* Integrates Somatic Experiencing with attachment repair to release chronic hypervigilance and relational shutdown stored in the nervous system.
18. **018. Love Me, Don't Leave Me** — Michelle Skeen (2014) | *TC:* 愛我，別離開我 | *SC:* 爱我，别离开我
    *Core Premise:* Addresses core schemas of abandonment, defectiveness, and mistrust that sabotage intimacy before closeness can take root.
19. **019. Facing Love Addiction** — Pia Mellody (1992) | *TC:* 直面愛情成癮 | *SC:* 直面爱情成瘾
    *Core Premise:* Dissects the obsessive cycle between "Love Addicts" who crave fusion and "Love Avoidants" who fear engulfment.
20. **020. The Intimacy Factor** — Pia Mellody (2003) | *TC:* 親密要素 | *SC:* 亲密要素
    *Core Premise:* Outlines the five relational skills—self-esteem, boundaries, reality awareness, self-care, and moderation—needed for healthy bonding.
21. **021. A General Theory of Love** — Thomas Lewis, Fari Amini & Richard Lannon (2000) | *TC:* 愛的普遍理論 | *SC:* 爱的普遍理论
    *Core Premise:* Explains limbic resonance, limbic regulation, and limbic revision—how human nervous systems literally attune to and reshape one another.
22. **022. Social: Why Our Brains Are Wired to Connect** — Matthew D. Lieberman (2013) | *TC:* 社交天性 | *SC:* 社交天性
    *Core Premise:* Demonstrates via fMRI science that social connection is a primary mammalian need on par with food and shelter, and rejection activates physical pain circuits.
23. **023. The Molecule of More** — Daniel Z. Lieberman & Michael E. Long (2018) | *TC:* 貪婪的多巴胺 | *SC:* 贪婪的多巴胺
    *Core Premise:* Contrasts the dopamine of romantic infatuation and novelty with the "Here-and-Now" neurotransmitters (oxytocin, vasopressin, endorphins) of enduring love.
24. **024. Oxytocin Factor** — Kerstin Uvnäs Moberg (2003) | *TC:* 催產素效應 | *SC:* 催产素效应
    *Core Premise:* Explores the biology of calm and connection, showing how touch, warmth, and emotional safety lower cortisol and deepen pair bonding.
25. **025. Strange Situation** — Bethany Saltman (2020) | *TC:* 陌生情境 | *SC:* 陌生情境
    *Core Premise:* Investigates Mary Ainsworth's groundbreaking attachment research alongside a candid personal exploration of delight, presence, and emotional security.

## Category 02: COMMUNICATION, CONFLICT & REPAIR (`溝通藝術、衝突與修復 /`)

26. **026. The Seven Principles for Making Marriage Work** — John Gottman & Nan Silver (1999) | *TC:* 幸福的婚姻 | *SC:* 幸福的婚姻
    *Core Premise:* Distills forty years of Love Lab research into actionable habits: Love Maps, turning toward bids, accepting influence, and repairing conflict.
27. **027. Nonviolent Communication** — Marshall B. Rosenberg (1999) | *TC:* 非暴力溝通 | *SC:* 非暴力沟通
    *Core Premise:* Replaces blame and moralistic judgment with the four-step compassionate framework of Observation, Feeling, Need, and Request.
28. **028. Getting the Love You Want** — Harville Hendrix (1988) | *TC:* 找回你想要的愛 | *SC:* 找回你想要的爱
    *Core Premise:* Introduces Imago Relationship Therapy and the structured Imago Dialogue (Mirroring, Validation, Empathy) to heal unconscious childhood projections.
29. **029. Fight Right** — Julie Schwartz Gottman & John Gottman (2024) | *TC:* 好好吵架 | *SC:* 好好吵架
    *Core Premise:* Refutes the myth that happy couples never fight, teaching partners how to navigate perpetual gridlock with gentle start-ups and repair attempts.
30. **030. Us: Getting Past You and Me to Build a More Loving Relationship** — Terrence Real (2022) | *TC:* 我們 | *SC:* 我们
    *Core Premise:* Challenges hyper-individualistic "me-first" culture, offering relational mindfulness tools to shift from righteous ego defense to collaborative "us" consciousness.
31. **031. Fierce Intimacy** — Terrence Real (2018) | *TC:* 熾熱親密 | *SC:* 炽热亲密
    *Core Premise:* Teaches how to stand up to a partner with loving clarity ("standing in your truth without losing your heart") rather than retreating into silence or rage.
32. **032. Crucial Conversations** — Kerry Patterson et al. (2002) | *TC:* 關鍵對話 | *SC:* 关键对话
    *Core Premise:* Provides tools for maintaining psychological safety and mutual purpose when stakes are high, opinions vary, and emotions run strong.
33. **033. Difficult Conversations** — Douglas Stone, Bruce Patton & Sheila Heen (1999) | *TC:* 高難度對話 | *SC:* 高难度对话
    *Core Premise:* Separates every argument into three hidden layers—the "What Happened" story, the Feelings conversation, and the Identity conversation.
34. **034. What Makes Love Last?** — John Gottman & Nan Silver (2012) | *TC:* 什麼讓愛長久？ | *SC:* 什么让爱长久？
    *Core Premise:* Defines trust as choosing your partner's well-being over your own self-interest and details how attunement prevents emotional betrayal.
35. **035. The Relationship Cure** — John Gottman & Joan DeClaire (2001) | *TC:* 關係療癒 | *SC:* 关系疗愈
    *Core Premise:* Focuses on "Emotional Bids"—the tiny daily requests for attention and affection whose acceptance or rejection determines relationship survival.
36. **036. Emotional Intelligence in Couples Therapy** — Brent J. Atkinson (2005) | *TC:* 伴侶治療中的情緒智慧 | *SC:* 伴侣治疗中的情绪智慧
    *Core Premise:* Integrates affective neuroscience to show how neural habits of defensiveness can be rewired through emotional self-regulation during conflict.
37. **037. Love Is Never Enough** — Aaron T. Beck (1988) | *TC:* 光有愛是不夠的 | *SC:* 光有爱是不够的
    *Core Premise:* Applies Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) to decode mind-reading, catastrophic assumptions, and invisible miscommunications between partners.
38. **038. We Need to Talk** — Celeste Headlee (2017) | *TC:* 我們需要談談 | *SC:* 我们需要谈谈
    *Core Premise:* Dismantles digital distraction and performative listening, restoring the lost art of curious, judgment-free human conversation.
39. **039. You're Not Listening** — Kate Murphy (2020) | *TC:* 你都沒在聽 | *SC:* 你都没在听
    *Core Premise:* Reveals how curiosity and sustained attention allow us to truly know the people closest to us rather than merely waiting for our turn to speak.
40. **040. I Hear You** — Michael S. Sorensen (2017) | *TC:* 我聽見你了 | *SC:* 我听见你了
    *Core Premise:* Demonstrates how pure emotional validation—acknowledging a partner's feelings before offering logic or solutions—instantly diffuses relational tension.
41. **041. Say What You Mean** — Oren Jay Sofer (2018) | *TC:* 說出你的真心話 | *SC:* 说出你的真心话
    *Core Premise:* Blends mindfulness, Nonviolent Communication, and somatic awareness into three steps: Lead with Presence, Come from Curiosity, and Focus on What Matters.
42. **042. The Five Love Languages** — Gary Chapman (1992) | *TC:* 愛的五種語言 | *SC:* 爱的五种语言
    *Core Premise:* Explains how couples miss each other's affection when speaking mismatched emotional dialects (Words, Quality Time, Gifts, Acts of Service, Touch).
43. **043. Love Busters** — Willard F. Harley Jr. (1992) | *TC:* 愛情殺手 | *SC:* 爱情杀手
    *Core Premise:* Identifies five destructive habits—angry outbursts, disrespect, annoying habits, independent behavior, and dishonesty—that drain the "Love Bank."
44. **044. His Needs, Her Needs** — Willard F. Harley Jr. (1986) | *TC:* 他的需求，她的需求 | *SC:* 他的需求，她的需求
    *Core Premise:* Maps the top emotional and practical needs partners must consistently fulfill to protect intimacy from drift and resentment.
45. **045. Couple Skills** — Matthew McKay, Patrick Fanning & Kim Paleg (1994) | *TC:* 伴侶溝通技巧 | *SC:* 伴侣沟通技巧
    *Core Premise:* Evidence-based cognitive and behavioral exercises for active listening, clean anger expression, and collaborative problem-solving.
46. **046. Eight Dates** — John Gottman, Julie Schwartz Gottman, Doug Abrams & Rachel Carlton Abrams (2019) | *TC:* 八場約會 | *SC:* 八场约会
    *Core Premise:* Structures eight essential conversations around trust, conflict, sex, money, family, fun, growth, and dreams to deepen intimacy.
47. **047. The High-Conflict Couple** — Alan E. Fruzzetti (2006) | *TC:* 高衝突伴侶 | *SC:* 高冲突伴侣
    *Core Premise:* Adapts Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) to help emotionally reactive partners de-escalate volatility and practice radical acceptance.
48. **048. After the Fight** — Daniel B. Wile (1993) | *TC:* 爭吵之後 | *SC:* 争吵之后
    *Core Premise:* Introduces "Collaborative Couple Therapy," turning post-argument regret into intimate conversations about the unspoken vulnerability behind the fight.
49. **049. Solving the Intimacy Puzzle** — Robert Taibbi (2018) | *TC:* 解開親密難題 | *SC:* 解开亲密难题
    *Core Premise:* Shows couples how to move beyond repetitive surface complaints to address underlying structural imbalances in power and emotional labor.
50. **050. Thanks for the Feedback** — Douglas Stone & Sheila Heen (2014) | *TC:* 謝謝你的指教 | *SC:* 谢谢你的指教
    *Core Premise:* Explains why receiving constructive criticism from a partner triggers truth, relationship, and identity alarms—and how to metabolize feedback gracefully.

## Category 03: SEX, DESIRE & EROTICISM (`性愛、慾望與情慾美學 /`)
