# Product brief — relationship & intimacy reader

Locked from Martin’s tap-through answers, 6 Nov 2026 (HKT). Visual locked: **Soft Uncanny** on Luminary’s chassis, type and grain only — [five screens](./soft-uncanny-luminary.md). No prototype until asked.

## What it is

A **reading sanctuary** for relationship and intimacy books only — Luminary’s concept, narrower canon. It may also serve as a quiet therapy companion and dating education, but it must never feel like a **dating app** or a **church pamphlet**.

- **Primary reader:** all of them (in a relationship, dating, after a breakup, therapists).
- **Exclusions:** none hard.
- **Home:** daily ritual on top of a full library.
- **Logs:** solo only. Every practice works for one person alone.

## Content

- **Scope:** romantic partnership, sex and desire, family-of-origin as it scripts pairing. Friendship / queer / CNM / parenting-as-partnership were not selected.
- **Extra-care topics:** none flagged for special handling; stay **strictly editorial** on abuse/crisis (no in-product resources).
- **Culture:** West + East pairing, like Luminary.
- **Language:** bilingual EN + 繁/简 for editorial text. Navigation chrome is English only.
- **Writing:** original editorial (commentary, practices, short quotes) — not licensed summaries.
- **Volume shape:** Luminary spine (thesis, 3 highlights, extras, practice).
- **Voice:** warm and intimate.
- **Canon:** [Relationship books 250](./relationship-books-250.md). Must-ship first is 1–30. Only *The Courage to Be Disliked* is carried over from Luminary; the rest of Luminary’s 15 and any non-pairing self-help are out. List tightened 6 Nov 2026 after Martin asked for relationship books only.

## Product

- **Membership:** free daily + Pro library. No special paywall refusals.
- **Accounts and sync:** from day one.
- **Persist:** read status, practice checkbox, private journal, weekly review, Markdown export (Obsidian / Notion). No couple journal, no streaks.
- **Device:** phone, mobile-first.
- **Journal privacy:** cloud sync is fine if notes stay out of the feed.
- **Disclaimer:** not therapy — quiet (footer / first-run only).

## Design

- **Mix:** Soft Uncanny (Surrealism × Pointillism) with Luminary’s editorial framework. Locked screens: [soft-uncanny-luminary.md](./soft-uncanny-luminary.md). Parked: [Design directions](./design-directions.md), [Clay Sanctuary](./clay-sanctuary-variations.md), other [new mixes](./new-mixes.md) (origin still `new-mix-04.png` still has a cup — not the product lock).
- **Feel:** primary **sensual but not sexual**; secondary **ritual / sacred**.
- **Imagery:** type, grain, and structure only. No faces, photos, cups, doors, keys, moons, or other pictures.
